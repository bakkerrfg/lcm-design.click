Services

What we offer

Design services

Choose a single stage or a full cycle. Each engagement is covered by a written agreement describing deliverables, timeline and cost.

01

Interior design project

Analysis of the brief, space planning options, moodboards and style concept, 3D visualizations, and a complete set of working drawings: layouts, electrical and lighting plans, ceiling and floor plans, joinery and details.

02

Furnishing and decoration

Selection of furniture, lighting, textiles, art and decor. We prepare specifications with suppliers and prices, and help coordinate orders and delivery.

03

Author supervision

Scheduled visits to the site, answers to contractors’ questions and checks that materials and details match the approved design.

04

Commercial interiors

Offices, showrooms, cafés and boutiques: layouts that support workflow and customer experience while reflecting your brand.

Deliverables

What you receive

  • Space planning options and final layout
  • Moodboards and style concept
  • 3D visualizations of key rooms
  • Working drawings and specifications
  • Furniture and materials schedules
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does a project start?

Send us a short description of your space and goals. We will reply by email and arrange a discussion to prepare a proposal.

How long does a design project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the space. We agree on a timeline in the contract before starting.

Do you work with commercial spaces?

Yes, we design offices, retail and hospitality interiors as well as residential projects.

Can I order only part of the services?

Yes. You can order a concept only, documentation, furnishing or supervision separately.
